2. The program

When you request a free cleaning estimate from our location through The Cleaning Authority estimate form (https://estimate.thecleaningauthority.com/) and check the optional SMS consent box, we may send you recurring text messages to follow up on your request. Messages may include a check-in on your estimate request, answers to your questions, questions about your home so we can prepare a quote, help scheduling a cleaning, and occasional offers for new customers from our location. Messages may be sent using automated technology. Some replies are generated by an automated assistant, with members of our local team involved whenever needed.

3. Consent

Consent to receive text messages is optional and is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services. You can request an estimate and become a customer without agreeing to receive texts. We text only phone numbers whose owners checked the SMS consent box, and we keep a record of the date and source of each opt-in.

Message frequency varies based on your conversation with us. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier's plan. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

5. How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts from us. You will receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed. You can also opt out by calling (954) 505-8013. Reply START to resubscribe.

6. How to get help
